About SG Salary Tools

Simple salary tools for Singapore employees

SG Salary Tools helps employees, job seekers, HR users and employers estimate salary, CPF, income tax, leave and employment-related figures more clearly.

SG Salary Tools was created to make Singapore salary planning easier to understand. Many people know their gross salary, but they may not immediately know how CPF, estimated income tax, bonuses, leave entitlement or other employment factors affect their final take-home pay.

This website brings common Singapore salary calculators and practical guides into one place, so users can quickly estimate numbers before checking official sources or speaking with payroll, HR, tax agents or financial advisers.

The calculators and guides on this website are for general information and estimation only. They are not official CPF, tax, legal, HR or financial advice.
