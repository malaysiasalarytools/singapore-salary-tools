About SG Salary Tools

SG Salary Tools is an independent online platform created to help people in Singapore better understand salary, CPF contributions, income tax, leave entitlement and employment-related calculations.

Why We Built SG Salary Tools

Many employees in Singapore know their gross monthly salary, but may not fully understand how their take-home pay is affected by CPF contributions, income tax, bonuses, tax reliefs and employment rules. Salary information can also be confusing when users need to compare job offers, estimate annual tax, check CPF contribution rates or understand leave entitlement.

SG Salary Tools was created to bring these topics together in one simple and easy-to-use platform. Our goal is to help users make quick estimates, understand common salary terms and learn about Singapore employment-related matters in a clear and practical way.

We focus on building free calculators and beginner-friendly guides for Singapore employees, job seekers, HR users, payroll teams, employers and anyone who wants to understand CPF, tax and salary planning more clearly.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Singapore salary, CPF, tax and employment information easier to understand for everyday users.

We believe users should be able to estimate take-home pay, compare salary packages, understand CPF deductions and learn basic employment concepts without needing advanced payroll or tax knowledge.

Information Sources

SG Salary Tools is not a government website. However, where applicable, our calculators and guides are prepared with reference to publicly available information from relevant Singapore authorities and official sources.

Common official references include:

  • CPF Board for CPF contribution rates, wage ceilings and CPF-related rules
  • IRAS for personal income tax, tax residency, tax relief and filing matters
  • Ministry of Manpower for employment, leave entitlement and workplace guidance
  • SingStat and other public sources for Singapore salary and labour market context
