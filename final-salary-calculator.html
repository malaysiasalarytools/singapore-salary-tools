How this final salary calculator works

Final salary is usually affected by how many days you worked in your final month, whether you took unpaid leave, whether unused annual leave is encashed, and whether there are other allowances or deductions.

1. Calculate daily salary

The calculator divides monthly salary by working days, calendar days or your company’s custom divisor.

2. Add salary for days worked

Paid days worked are multiplied by daily salary to estimate your prorated final month salary.

3. Add or deduct adjustments

Unused leave, unpaid leave, allowances and deductions are added to estimate final gross salary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate final salary in Singapore?

A simple estimate is salary for days worked plus unused annual leave encashment and other pay, minus unpaid leave deduction and other deductions.

Is final salary based on working days or calendar days?

It depends on your employment contract and company payroll policy. Some employers use working days, while others may use calendar days or another agreed method.

Can unused annual leave be paid out when I resign?

Unused annual leave may be encashed depending on the Employment Act, your employment contract and company policy. You should confirm your leave balance and payout treatment with HR.

Does this calculator include CPF?

No. This calculator estimates final gross salary before CPF. Use the CPF Calculator to estimate CPF on salary items.

Can I use this for resignation salary?

Yes. This calculator is designed for employees who resign, leave mid-month or want to estimate their final payslip.

Is this final salary calculator official?

No. It is an independent estimate for planning only. For official employment matters, check MOM guidance or your employer’s HR policy.
