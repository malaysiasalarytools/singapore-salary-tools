How this take-home pay calculator works

Take-home pay is the estimated cash amount you receive after salary deductions. In Singapore, the main deductions usually include employee CPF contribution, income tax allocation and any other company or personal deductions.

1. Start with gross salary

Your gross monthly salary is the amount before CPF, tax and other deductions.

2. Deduct employee CPF

For eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs, employee CPF reduces monthly cash salary.

3. Estimate monthly net pay

The calculator allocates annual income tax over 12 months and subtracts other monthly deductions.

Common take-home pay scenarios

These examples help users understand how different situations affect monthly net salary.

CPF eligible employee

If you are a Singapore Citizen or PR third year onwards, employee CPF is deducted from salary. Employer CPF is paid separately and does not reduce your take-home pay.

Foreigner without CPF

Many foreigners do not contribute CPF. Their take-home pay may mainly be affected by income tax and other deductions.

Bonus allocation

If you want a monthly planning view, you can allocate annual bonus across 12 months. If not, keep bonus excluded from monthly cash pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is take-home pay in Singapore?

Take-home pay is the estimated amount you receive after employee CPF, income tax allocation and other deductions.

Does this calculator include CPF?

Yes. It estimates employee CPF and employer CPF for eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs. You can also select no CPF if CPF does not apply.

Is employer CPF deducted from my salary?

No. Employer CPF is paid separately by the employer. It does not reduce your monthly take-home pay.

Is income tax deducted monthly in Singapore?

Singapore income tax is usually assessed annually. This calculator spreads estimated annual tax across 12 months for planning.

Why is my actual take-home pay different?

Actual payroll may differ because of CPF treatment, tax reliefs, unpaid leave, benefits, bonuses, reimbursements and company deductions.

Can foreigners use this calculator?

Yes. Foreigners can select the no CPF option to estimate take-home pay without CPF deduction.

Should I include bonus in monthly take-home pay?

If you want a conservative monthly cash estimate, exclude bonus. If you want an annual planning view, allocate bonus across 12 months.

Is this calculator official?

No. This calculator provides estimates only. For official confirmation, refer to CPF Board, IRAS or your employer.
