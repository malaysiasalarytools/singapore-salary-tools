Singapore AWS and bonus guide for 2026

13th Month Bonus Singapore Guide 2026

Learn what the 13th month bonus means in Singapore, whether Annual Wage Supplement is compulsory, how AWS may be calculated, and how CPF, income tax, resignation and pro-ration can affect your payment.

Also Called AWS Usually Up to 1 Month Not Automatically Compulsory CPF & Tax May Apply

Quick answer

The 13th month bonus in Singapore is commonly known as the Annual Wage Supplement, or AWS. It is a single annual payment on top of an employee’s regular annual wages. AWS is generally not required by law unless it is stated in the employment contract, company handbook or collective agreement.

What is the 13th month bonus in Singapore?

A 13th month bonus is an additional annual payment that is commonly linked to one month of salary. In Singapore, it is more formally known as the Annual Wage Supplement. It is usually paid near the end of the year, although the actual payment month depends on the employer’s policy and employment terms.

Annual Wage Supplement

AWS is the common Singapore term for the 13th month payment. It is paid in addition to the employee’s normal monthly wages.

One annual payment

It is usually paid once a year. Many employees receive it around November, December or the following payroll cycle.

Terms matter

Eligibility, amount, payment date and pro-ration depend mainly on the employment contract, handbook or collective agreement.

Is the 13th month bonus compulsory in Singapore?

For most employees, AWS is not automatically compulsory. The employer normally has to pay it only when the employee is entitled to it under the relevant employment terms.

Important: A performance bonus and AWS are not automatically the same thing. A company may provide AWS, a performance bonus, both payments or neither payment, depending on the employment terms.

Employees often use “13th month bonus”, “AWS” and “year-end bonus” interchangeably, but the payments can have different purposes.

How is the 13th month bonus calculated?

There is no single calculation that applies to every employee. The contract may provide one full month of basic salary, one month of another salary definition, or a pro-rated amount based on completed service.

Full-year employee

If the contract provides one month of basic salary and the employee served the full qualifying year, AWS may equal one month of basic salary.

Joined during the year

The payment may be pro-rated according to completed months or days of service, but only if the employment terms provide for it.

Salary changed

The employer may use last-drawn basic salary, average salary or another contractual formula. Check the exact wording.

Simple pro-rated AWS formula

Estimated AWS = Monthly salary used by the company × Eligible months of service ÷ 12. This is a planning example only. Your employer may use a different formula under the contract or company policy.

Is CPF payable on the 13th month bonus?

Cash bonuses paid to eligible Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents generally attract CPF contributions. A 13th month payment is normally treated as Additional Wages for CPF purposes and is subject to the Additional Wage ceiling.

Employee CPF

The employee’s CPF share may be deducted from the AWS, reducing the cash amount received.

Employer CPF

The employer generally contributes its share separately, subject to the applicable CPF rate and wage ceiling.

Additional Wage ceiling

For 2026, the CPF Additional Wage ceiling is generally S$102,000 minus total Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year.

CPF treatment depends on citizenship or PR status, age, wage level, the amount of Ordinary Wages already subject to CPF and the applicable Additional Wage ceiling. Use the official CPF calculator for an exact payroll computation.

Is the 13th month bonus taxable?

Yes. AWS and other employment bonuses are generally taxable employment income in Singapore. A contractual 13th month payment is normally taxed according to when the employee becomes entitled to it under the contract.

Taxable employment income

The gross bonus generally forms part of your employment income for Singapore income tax purposes.

Before CPF deduction

Employment income is generally reported before deducting the employee’s CPF contribution.

AIS employers

If your employer is under the Auto-Inclusion Scheme, the employment income information is generally submitted to IRAS for you.

Do you get AWS if you resign?

Resigning before the AWS payment date does not create one universal result. Your entitlement depends on the written employment terms. Some employers pro-rate AWS, while others require the employee to remain employed on the payment date.

Contract provides pro-ration

You may receive a pro-rated payment based on eligible service, subject to the formula and conditions in the contract.

Must be employed on payment date

Some terms require the employee to still be employed and not serving notice when the AWS is paid.

No resignation clause

Review the full contract, handbook and past written communications. Ask HR to confirm the company’s treatment in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 13th month bonus in Singapore?

It is commonly known as the Annual Wage Supplement or AWS. It is a single annual payment made on top of the employee’s normal annual wages.

Is the 13th month bonus compulsory in Singapore?

Generally, no. AWS is not automatically required by law. It is usually payable only when the employment contract, company handbook or collective agreement provides for it.

Is AWS the same as a performance bonus?

No. AWS is commonly an additional annual wage payment, while a performance bonus is usually linked to individual, team or company performance. An employer may pay both.

How much is the 13th month bonus?

It is often described as one month of salary, but the exact amount and salary base depend on the employment terms. It may also be pro-rated for employees who did not serve the full qualifying year.

Is CPF payable on the 13th month bonus?

CPF is generally payable on cash bonuses for eligible Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents. AWS is normally treated as Additional Wages and is subject to the Additional Wage ceiling.

Is the 13th month bonus taxable in Singapore?

Yes. AWS and other employment bonuses are generally taxable employment income. The relevant tax year for a contractual bonus depends on when the employee becomes entitled to it under the contract.

Do foreigners receive a 13th month bonus?

Foreign employees may receive AWS if their employment terms provide for it. CPF generally does not apply to foreign employees, but Singapore income tax may still apply depending on the employee’s tax position.

Do I get AWS if I joined during the year?

It depends on the contract or company policy. Some employers provide a pro-rated AWS, while others impose a minimum service period or require the employee to be confirmed.

Is probation included when calculating AWS?

There is no universal rule. The employment contract, company handbook or collective agreement determines whether probation counts toward AWS eligibility and pro-ration.

Do I get AWS if I resign before December?

It depends on the written conditions. Some employers pay a pro-rated AWS, while others require the employee to remain employed on the payment date or not be serving notice.

Can an employer reduce AWS?

MOM states that an employer can negotiate a lower AWS if business results are exceptionally poor. The employer should also consider any contractual or collective agreement obligations.

Is this 13th month bonus guide official?

No. SG Salary Tools provides general salary and employment planning information only. For exact entitlement, check your contract and the latest guidance from MOM, CPF Board and IRAS.
