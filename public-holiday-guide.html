What happens if a public holiday falls on Sunday?

In Singapore, when a public holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday may be observed as a public holiday if Sunday is the employee’s rest day. In 2026, Vesak Day, National Day and Deepavali fall on Sunday.

Public holiday pay in Singapore

Employees covered by the Employment Act are generally entitled to paid public holidays. The exact treatment depends on whether the employee works on the public holiday, whether it falls on a rest day, and the employment contract.

Paid public holiday

If you are entitled to the public holiday and you do not work on that day, you are generally paid for the holiday.

Working on a public holiday

If you are required to work on a public holiday, you may be entitled to extra salary or a substitute day off, depending on the applicable rules.

Holiday during leave

If a public holiday falls during annual leave, sick leave or other leave, the treatment may depend on MOM rules and your employment arrangement.

Can employers make employees work on public holidays?

Some businesses need employees to work on public holidays, especially in retail, hospitality, healthcare, logistics, customer service and operations roles. When this happens, employers should handle public holiday pay and time off correctly.

Retail and F&B

Employees may be scheduled during peak public holiday periods. The employer should communicate the roster clearly.

Shift workers

Public holiday treatment can be more complex for shift workers because the holiday may overlap with rest days or rostered days.

Office workers

Many office employees do not work on public holidays. If work is required, the arrangement should be agreed and documented.

Public holiday vs annual leave

Public holidays and annual leave are different. Public holidays are gazetted holidays, while annual leave is the employee’s personal leave entitlement. Employees often combine annual leave with public holidays to create longer breaks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many public holidays does Singapore have in 2026?

Singapore has 11 gazetted public holidays in 2026, including New Year's Day, Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Good Friday, Labour Day, Hari Raya Haji, Vesak Day, National Day, Deepavali and Christmas Day.

What are the Singapore public holidays in 2026?

The 2026 public holidays include 1 January, 17 and 18 February, 21 March, 3 April, 1 May, 27 May, 31 May, 9 August, 8 November and 25 December.

What happens when a public holiday falls on Sunday in Singapore?

If a public holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday may be observed as a public holiday if Sunday is your rest day. In 2026, Vesak Day, National Day and Deepavali fall on Sunday.

Do employees get paid for public holidays in Singapore?

Employees covered by the Employment Act are generally entitled to paid public holidays. The exact treatment depends on employment terms, work schedule and whether the employee works on that day.

What if I have to work on a public holiday?

If you are required to work on a public holiday, you may be entitled to extra salary or a substitute day off, depending on the applicable rules and your employment arrangement.

Can I use annual leave to extend a public holiday weekend?

Yes. Many employees use annual leave around public holidays to create longer breaks. However, annual leave is usually subject to employer approval.

Is Chinese New Year a public holiday in Singapore?

Yes. Chinese New Year is a public holiday in Singapore. In 2026, Chinese New Year falls on Tuesday, 17 February and Wednesday, 18 February.

Is National Day a public holiday in Singapore?

Yes. National Day is a public holiday in Singapore. In 2026, National Day falls on Sunday, 9 August, with Monday, 10 August potentially observed if Sunday is your rest day.
