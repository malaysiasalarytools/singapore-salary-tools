What Happens If A Public Holiday Falls On Sunday?

If a Singapore public holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday will normally be a public holiday.

Do Employees Get Paid On Public Holidays?

Employees covered under Singapore employment laws are entitled to paid public holidays.

What If I Work On A Public Holiday?

Employees may be entitled to an additional day's salary or another day off according to MOM regulations.
