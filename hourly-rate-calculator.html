How this Singapore hourly rate calculator works

This calculator converts monthly salary into annual salary, weekly pay, daily rate and hourly rate. It is useful when comparing monthly salary with hourly jobs, part-time work, overtime pay or freelance rates.

1. Annualise salary

Monthly salary is multiplied by 12 to estimate annual salary.

2. Estimate yearly hours

Weekly working hours are multiplied by 52 weeks to estimate annual working hours.

3. Calculate hourly rate

Annual salary is divided by annual working hours to estimate hourly rate.

Hourly rate formula in Singapore

For a monthly-rated employee, a common formula for hourly basic rate is:

Item Formula Example
Annual salary Monthly salary × 12 S$5,000 × 12 = S$60,000
Annual working hours Weekly working hours × 52 44 × 52 = 2,288 hours
Hourly rate Annual salary ÷ annual working hours S$60,000 ÷ 2,288 = about S$26.22 per hour
Daily rate estimate Annual salary ÷ annual working days S$60,000 ÷ 286 = about S$209.79 per day
Basic salary and gross salary may produce different hourly rates. For overtime calculation, basic rate of pay may be required.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate hourly rate from monthly salary in Singapore?

A common estimate is: hourly rate = monthly salary × 12 ÷ annual working hours. If you work 44 hours per week, annual working hours are 44 × 52 = 2,288 hours.

What is the hourly rate for S$5,000 monthly salary?

If you earn S$5,000 per month and work 44 hours per week, the estimated hourly rate is about S$26.22 per hour.

Should I use basic salary or gross salary?

For overtime and basic rate calculations, basic salary may be more relevant. For general salary comparison, gross salary can be useful.

Is hourly rate the same as overtime rate?

No. Hourly rate is the base hourly value of your salary. Overtime rate is usually higher, often calculated as hourly basic rate multiplied by the overtime multiplier.

Can I use this for part-time salary comparison?

Yes. You can use this calculator to compare a monthly salary with an hourly or part-time rate. Actual employment terms may still differ.

Is this calculator official?

No. This calculator provides estimates only. For official salary and payroll calculations, refer to MOM guidance or your employment contract.
