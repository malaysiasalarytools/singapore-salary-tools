Singapore IR8A guide for employees and employers

Learn what Form IR8A is, what employment income is reported, how AIS works, and how IR8A affects employee income tax filing in Singapore.

Employment Income AIS Submission Tax Filing CPF & Benefits

What is Form IR8A?

Form IR8A is a Singapore employment income form prepared by employers. It reports an employee’s income for the year, such as salary, bonus, allowances, benefits and other employment-related payments.

Employees usually use IR8A information when checking their annual income tax filing. Employers may submit the employment income information directly to IRAS under the Auto-Inclusion Scheme, also known as AIS.

Simple rule: IR8A is about employment income reporting. It helps IRAS and employees understand how much employment income was earned during the year.

Who needs Form IR8A?

Employers generally prepare IR8A information for employees who received employment income during the year. This can include full-time employees, part-time employees, directors and other employees who received taxable employment income.

If the employer is under AIS, the employer submits the employment income details electronically to IRAS. Employees can usually view pre-filled income information when filing their personal income tax return.

What is AIS?

AIS means Auto-Inclusion Scheme. Under AIS, employers submit employees’ employment income information directly to IRAS. This means the income details are automatically included in the employee’s tax assessment system.

For employees, AIS can make tax filing easier because employment income may already be pre-filled. However, employees should still check that the income details are correct before submitting their tax return.

Important: Even if your employer submits under AIS, you should still review your tax filing carefully. Pre-filled does not mean you should ignore errors.

What income is shown in IR8A?

IR8A can include different types of employment income. The exact items depend on what the employee received during the year.

IR8A Item What It Means Example
Salary Regular employment salary earned during the year. Monthly salary from January to December
Bonus Bonus, AWS, performance bonus or other incentive payments. 13th month payment or annual bonus
Allowances Employment allowances paid to the employee. Transport allowance, meal allowance or fixed allowance
Director’s fees Fees paid to company directors. Director fee approved and paid
Benefits-in-kind Taxable non-cash benefits provided by the employer. Housing benefit, car benefit or other staff benefits
Employee CPF Employee CPF contributions relevant to tax relief reporting. Employee CPF deducted from salary

IR8A, IR8S, Appendix 8A and Appendix 8B

IR8A is the main employment income form, but some employees may have additional forms depending on their employment benefits or CPF situation.

Form Purpose Common Situation
Form IR8A Reports employment income for the year. Salary, bonus, allowances and employment income.
Form IR8S Reports excess or voluntary CPF contributions where applicable. Additional CPF-related reporting situations.
Appendix 8A Reports benefits-in-kind. Housing, car or other taxable employee benefits.
Appendix 8B Reports gains or profits from employee share options or share ownership plans. Stock options, share plans or employee equity benefits.

What employees should check

Employees should compare IR8A income with payslips, CPF records, bonus letters and bank payments. This helps identify missing bonus, wrong salary period, incorrect allowance classification or CPF reporting errors.

  • Check your total salary for the year.
  • Check whether bonus and AWS are included correctly.
  • Check allowances and benefits-in-kind.
  • Check CPF-related information.
  • Check whether your employer submitted under AIS.
  • Check your IRAS pre-filled income before filing.

What employers should prepare

Employers should keep accurate payroll records throughout the year so IR8A information can be prepared correctly. Payroll errors can affect employees’ tax filing and may create follow-up questions from employees or IRAS.

Useful records include monthly payroll summaries, payslips, CPF contribution records, bonus approvals, director fee approvals, benefit records and staff movement records.

When is IR8A submitted?

Employers under AIS generally submit employment income information to IRAS after the year ends and before the tax filing season. Employees should check the latest IRAS deadline each year because filing dates and administrative requirements may change.

For salary planning content, it is safer to tell users to confirm the latest AIS and IR8A deadline directly with IRAS instead of relying only on an old saved date.

How IR8A connects with CPF and tax

IR8A is for income tax reporting, while CPF contribution is reported and paid under CPF rules. However, both are connected because salary, bonus and CPF contributions affect an employee’s tax position.

Employee CPF may be relevant for CPF relief, while bonus and allowances may increase taxable employment income. This is why employees should compare payslip, CPF and IR8A information together.

Common IR8A mistakes

Forgetting bonus

Bonus, AWS and incentives should be reviewed carefully when checking annual income.

Ignoring benefits

Benefits-in-kind may need separate reporting through Appendix 8A.

Not checking AIS data

Employees should still review pre-filled income before submitting tax filing.

Official sources to check

For official IR8A, AIS and employment income reporting rules, check IRAS directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IR8A in Singapore?

IR8A is a form used to report an employee’s employment income for income tax purposes in Singapore.

Is IR8A for employees or employers?

Employers prepare or submit IR8A information. Employees use the information to check employment income when filing income tax.

What is AIS?

AIS means Auto-Inclusion Scheme. Under AIS, employers submit employment income information directly to IRAS.

Do employees need to submit IR8A themselves?

If the employer submits through AIS, the employee’s employment income is usually pre-filled in the tax filing system. Employees should still check the details carefully.

What income is included in IR8A?

IR8A may include salary, bonus, allowances, director’s fees, benefits and other employment-related income.

What is Appendix 8A?

Appendix 8A is used for reporting benefits-in-kind, such as certain housing, car or other taxable employee benefits.

What is Appendix 8B?

Appendix 8B is used for reporting gains or profits from employee share options or share ownership plans.

What is IR8S?

IR8S is related to CPF reporting situations, such as excess or voluntary CPF contributions where applicable.

Should I check my IR8A against my payslips?

Yes. Employees should compare IR8A information with payslips, bonus letters, CPF records and bank payments.

Is this IR8A guide official tax advice?

No. This guide is for general salary and tax planning only. Check IRAS or a qualified tax adviser for official treatment.
