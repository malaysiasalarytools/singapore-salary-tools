Salary increment formulas

Salary increment can be calculated in different ways depending on what information you already know.

What you know Formula Example
Increment percentage New salary = Current salary × (1 + increment %) S$4,000 with 5% increment = S$4,200
Increment amount New salary = Current salary + increment amount S$4,000 + S$300 = S$4,300
New salary Increment % = Increase ÷ Current salary × 100 S$4,000 to S$4,500 = 12.5% increase
Salary increment percentage is usually calculated based on fixed monthly salary, not total annual compensation with bonus, commission or variable allowances.

What to consider before accepting a salary increment

A salary increase is useful, but the amount you actually receive in your bank account may be lower after CPF, tax and other payroll deductions.

CPF impact

Higher salary may increase employee CPF contribution, depending on CPF wage ceilings and eligibility.

Income tax impact

A higher annual income may increase estimated annual tax payable.

Total package

Compare basic salary, AWS, bonus, allowance, commission and benefits, not just monthly salary.

Increment percentage vs increment amount

Percentage increment helps compare raises across different salary levels, while dollar amount shows the actual increase you will receive each month.

Method Best for What it shows

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate salary increment percentage?

Salary increment percentage is calculated as: increment amount divided by current salary, multiplied by 100. For example, if your salary increases from S$4,000 to S$4,400, the increment is S$400, or 10%.

How do I calculate my new salary after increment?

If you know the increment percentage, multiply your current salary by 1 plus the increment percentage. For example, S$4,000 with a 5% increment becomes S$4,200.

Is salary increment calculated before or after CPF?

Salary increment is usually discussed based on gross monthly salary before employee CPF, tax and other deductions.

Does a salary increment increase CPF contribution?

It may increase CPF contribution if the employee is CPF-eligible and the salary increase affects the wage amount subject to CPF. CPF wage ceilings may also affect the final amount.

Does salary increment increase income tax?

A higher annual income may increase income tax payable, depending on total chargeable income, tax residency and available tax reliefs.

Should I calculate increment based on monthly or annual salary?

Most salary increments are calculated based on monthly basic salary. However, for career planning, it is useful to compare the annual increase as well.

Is AWS included in salary increment?

AWS or 13th month pay is usually a separate salary component. This calculator lets you view annual salary on a 12-month or 13-month basis for comparison.

Is this salary increment calculator official?

No. SG Salary Tools provides general planning calculators only. For exact payroll treatment, check your employment contract, HR policy or seek professional advice.
