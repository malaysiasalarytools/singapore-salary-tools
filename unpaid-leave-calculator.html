MOM no-pay leave calculation

A monthly-rated full-time employee who takes no-pay leave has an incomplete month of work. MOM calculates the payable salary using the monthly gross rate of pay divided by the total number of working days in that month, multiplied by days actually worked.

Monthly gross rate ÷ total working days in month × days actually worked
This calculator expresses the same rule as a no-pay leave deduction. Choose the employee's actual work schedule so the monthly working-day count is appropriate.

When is unpaid leave deduction used?

Unpaid leave deduction is usually used when an employee is not entitled to paid leave for the absence period, or when the employee applies for no-pay leave.

No-pay leave

The employee takes approved leave but the leave is not paid by the employer.

Unpaid absence

The employee is absent without paid leave entitlement or without sufficient leave balance.

Partial month payroll

Unpaid days may reduce the monthly salary payable for that month.

Why the number of working days matters

For a monthly-rated full-time employee on no-pay leave, MOM treats the month as an incomplete month of work. The denominator is the total number of working days in that month under the employee’s normal work schedule. Rest days and non-working days are excluded, while public holidays that fall on scheduled working days are included.

Do not substitute an arbitrary calendar-day or fixed 26-day divisor for the statutory incomplete-month formula.

Important notes before using the result

This calculator only estimates unpaid leave salary deduction. It does not decide whether unpaid leave should be approved, whether CPF applies, or whether other payroll adjustments are needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is unpaid leave?

Unpaid leave, also called no-pay leave, is leave where the employee is not paid for the leave period. The salary for the month may be reduced based on the number of unpaid days.

How do I calculate unpaid leave deduction in Singapore?

For a monthly-rated full-time employee, use the monthly gross rate divided by the total working days in that month, then apply the number of unpaid working days (or equivalently calculate salary for days actually worked).

Should unpaid leave be deducted based on calendar days or working days?

For the MOM statutory incomplete-month calculation, use the employee’s working days in the relevant month. A calendar-day or fixed 26-day shortcut should not replace that formula.

Can unpaid leave be half day?

Yes, if your employer allows half-day unpaid leave. You can enter 0.5 day in this calculator.

Does unpaid leave affect CPF?

This calculator does not calculate CPF. In practice, CPF is usually calculated based on the actual wages payable, so unpaid leave may reduce the wage amount used for CPF calculation.

Does unpaid leave affect annual leave?

It may depend on company policy and the length of unpaid leave. For long periods of unpaid leave, employers may have specific rules on leave accrual.

Is no-pay leave the same as unpaid leave?

In many workplace situations, no-pay leave and unpaid leave mean the same thing: approved leave where salary is not paid for the leave period.

Is this unpaid leave calculator official?

No. SG Salary Tools provides general planning calculators only. For exact payroll treatment, check your employment contract, HR policy or seek professional advice.
