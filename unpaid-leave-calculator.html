How this unpaid leave calculator works

Unpaid leave deduction formula

The general idea is to calculate a daily rate first, then multiply it by the number of unpaid leave days.

Basis Formula Common use
Calendar days Monthly salary ÷ calendar days in the month × unpaid leave days Useful when deduction is based on the full calendar month.
Working days Monthly salary ÷ working days in the month × unpaid leave days Useful when payroll counts only Monday to Friday working days.
Fixed 26-day basis Monthly salary ÷ 26 × unpaid leave days Commonly used as a simple daily rate salary deduction method.
Different companies may use different payroll formulas. The employment contract, staff handbook or HR policy should be followed for the actual deduction.

When is unpaid leave deduction used?

Unpaid leave deduction is usually used when an employee is not entitled to paid leave for the absence period, or when the employee applies for no-pay leave.

No-pay leave

The employee takes approved leave but the leave is not paid by the employer.

Unpaid absence

The employee is absent without paid leave entitlement or without sufficient leave balance.

Partial month payroll

Unpaid days may reduce the monthly salary payable for that month.

Calendar days vs working days vs 26 days

The unpaid leave deduction can be different depending on which basis your employer uses. This is why two employees with the same salary and same unpaid leave days may see different deductions under different payroll methods.

Calendar days

Divides salary by the number of days in the month, such as 28, 30 or 31 days.

Working days

Divides salary by working days in the month. This calculator counts Monday to Friday.

26-day basis

Divides salary by 26. This can produce a higher daily deduction than calendar basis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is unpaid leave?

Unpaid leave, also called no-pay leave, is leave where the employee is not paid for the leave period. The salary for the month may be reduced based on the number of unpaid days.

How do I calculate unpaid leave deduction in Singapore?

A common method is monthly salary divided by the payroll basis, multiplied by the number of unpaid leave days. The basis may be calendar days, working days or a fixed 26-day basis.

Should unpaid leave be deducted based on calendar days or working days?

It depends on your employment contract and company payroll policy. Calendar days, working days and 26-day basis can all give different results.

Can unpaid leave be half day?

Yes, if your employer allows half-day unpaid leave. You can enter 0.5 day in this calculator.

Does unpaid leave affect CPF?

This calculator does not calculate CPF. In practice, CPF is usually calculated based on the actual wages payable, so unpaid leave may reduce the wage amount used for CPF calculation.

Does unpaid leave affect annual leave?

It may depend on company policy and the length of unpaid leave. For long periods of unpaid leave, employers may have specific rules on leave accrual.

Is no-pay leave the same as unpaid leave?

In many workplace situations, no-pay leave and unpaid leave mean the same thing: approved leave where salary is not paid for the leave period.

Is this unpaid leave calculator official?

No. SG Salary Tools provides general planning calculators only. For exact payroll treatment, check your employment contract, HR policy or seek professional advice.
