Singapore bonus guide for employees

Understand how bonus, AWS, 13th month payment, CPF on bonus, income tax and prorated bonus work in Singapore. Useful for employees comparing job offers, planning resignation or checking year-end salary.

AWS / 13th Month Performance Bonus CPF on Bonus Bonus Tax Planning

Quick answer

A bonus in Singapore is an additional payment on top of basic salary. AWS, performance bonus, company bonus and retention bonus may be taxable employment income. CPF may also apply to bonus for eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs, subject to CPF wage ceiling rules.

What is a bonus in Singapore?

A bonus is an additional payment given to employees on top of their basic salary. It may be paid as a year-end bonus, performance bonus, company bonus, variable bonus, retention bonus or Annual Wage Supplement, commonly known as AWS or 13th month payment.

AWS / 13th month

A single annual payment on top of normal salary, usually equal to one month of basic salary if granted.

Performance bonus

A bonus linked to performance, appraisal rating, KPI, company results or management decision.

CPF and tax

Bonus may attract CPF for eligible employees and is usually counted as employment income for tax.

Is bonus compulsory in Singapore?

In Singapore, bonus payments are generally not automatic unless they are stated in your employment contract, collective agreement, company policy or official bonus letter. If the bonus is discretionary, the employer usually has more flexibility to decide whether to pay it and how much to pay.

Simple rule: Check your employment contract first. If the bonus or AWS is clearly promised in writing, it is stronger than a verbal expectation or past practice.

Common types of bonus in Singapore

Different bonuses can have different rules. Some are contractual, while others are discretionary or tied to company performance, appraisal outcomes or continued employment.

Bonus Type Meaning Usually Compulsory?
AWS / 13th Month Payment A single annual payment on top of basic annual salary. Only if stated in contract or collective agreement.
Performance Bonus Bonus based on employee performance, appraisal or KPI. Usually discretionary unless promised in writing.
Company Bonus Bonus based on company performance, profits or management decision. Usually discretionary.
Retention Bonus Bonus paid if employee remains employed until a required date. Depends on the retention bonus terms.

AWS vs performance bonus

AWS and performance bonus are often discussed together, but they are not exactly the same. AWS is commonly treated as a 13th month payment, while performance bonus usually depends on individual, department or company performance.

Is AWS or 13th month bonus compulsory?

AWS is also called the 13th month payment. It is a single annual payment made on top of an employee’s normal annual salary. AWS is not compulsory by default. It depends on what is stated in your employment contract, collective agreement or company policy.

Contract says AWS is payable

If your contract gives a clear AWS entitlement, your employer should follow the agreed terms.

Contract is silent

If the contract does not mention AWS, the company may still choose to pay it as discretionary bonus.

Example amount

If your monthly salary is S$4,000 and your company pays 1 month AWS, your AWS is usually S$4,000 before CPF and tax.

Is performance bonus compulsory?

Performance bonus is usually not compulsory unless the contract or bonus scheme gives you a clear entitlement. Many companies use wording such as “discretionary bonus”, “subject to company performance” or “subject to management approval”. This means the final amount may depend on employer decision.

If the bonus is clearly guaranteed in your offer letter or employment contract, keep a copy of that document because it helps support your claim if there is a dispute.

Is CPF payable on bonus?

CPF is generally payable on bonus for Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents who are eligible for CPF. Bonus is usually treated as Additional Wages for CPF purposes. This means it may be subject to the CPF Additional Wage ceiling.

Important CPF reminder

Bonus CPF is not always simply 20% of the full bonus. CPF wage ceilings can affect how much of the bonus attracts CPF contribution. Your exact CPF amount depends on age, CPF eligibility, Ordinary Wages and Additional Wage ceiling.

How bonus connects with CPF wage ceilings

Bonus is commonly treated as Additional Wages for CPF planning. The amount of bonus that attracts CPF can depend on how much Ordinary Wage you earned during the year and the applicable Additional Wage ceiling.

CPF Term Simple Meaning Why It Matters for Bonus
Ordinary Wages Regular monthly wages such as monthly salary. Your annual Ordinary Wages can affect the Additional Wage ceiling.
Additional Wages Non-regular wages such as bonus, AWS and annual incentive payments. Bonus CPF is usually checked under Additional Wage rules.
CPF wage ceiling A limit on wages that attract CPF contributions. Not every dollar of bonus necessarily attracts CPF if ceilings are reached.

Is bonus taxable in Singapore?

Bonus is generally part of employment income and may be taxable in Singapore. This includes AWS, performance bonus, incentive bonus and other employment-related bonus payments.

Tax residents

Bonus is usually added to annual employment income and taxed under resident progressive income tax rates.

Non-residents

Tax treatment may differ depending on tax residency, employment period and income type.

Annual tax impact

Bonus can increase annual chargeable income, which may increase your final income tax bill.

Do you get bonus if you resign?

Whether you receive bonus after resignation depends on the bonus terms. Some companies require employees to be in active employment on the bonus payment date. Others may require employees not to be serving notice at the time of bonus declaration or payout.

Guaranteed or discretionary

Check whether the bonus is guaranteed in writing or subject to management discretion.

Payout date condition

Some companies require you to remain employed until the bonus payout date.

Serving notice

Some bonus policies exclude employees who are serving notice when the bonus is declared or paid.

How does prorated bonus work?

Prorated bonus means the bonus is calculated based on the period you worked during the bonus year. This is common when employees join midway through the year, resign before year-end or are only eligible for part of the bonus period.

Simple prorated bonus formula

Monthly salary × bonus months × months worked ÷ 12. For example, if your salary is S$4,000, the company pays 1 month bonus, and you worked 9 months, a simple prorated bonus estimate is S$3,000.

Actual bonus calculation may be different if your company uses appraisal rating, company performance factor, bonus pool allocation or a specific HR policy.

Common bonus mistakes employees make

These are common mistakes employees make when planning for AWS, performance bonus, CPF on bonus and resignation timing.

Assuming bonus is automatic

Many bonuses are discretionary unless clearly stated in the employment contract or official bonus plan.

Ignoring CPF ceiling

CPF on bonus may be affected by Additional Wage ceiling rules, so the CPF amount may not be a simple flat percentage.

Resigning too early

Some companies require employees to remain employed until the bonus declaration or payout date.

Official sources to check

For official confirmation, always check Singapore government sources, your employment contract and your company’s bonus policy.

How a Singapore bonus affects CPF and tax

A bonus can be an Additional Wage for CPF purposes and may therefore be subject to the annual AW ceiling. For 2026, the AW ceiling is based on S$102,000 less total Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year. Separately, a taxable bonus forms part of annual employment income for income-tax purposes. AWS, 13th month payment and performance bonus are not identical

Frequently Asked Questions

Is bonus compulsory in Singapore?

Bonus is generally not compulsory unless it is stated in your employment contract, collective agreement or company policy.

Is AWS the same as 13th month bonus?

Yes. AWS is commonly known as the 13th month payment. It is a single annual payment on top of normal salary.

Is AWS compulsory in Singapore?

AWS is not compulsory by default. It depends on your employment contract, collective agreement or company policy.

Is CPF payable on bonus?

CPF is generally payable on bonus for CPF-eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs, subject to CPF wage ceiling rules.

Is bonus taxable in Singapore?

Bonus is generally treated as employment income and may be taxable as part of your annual income.

Do foreigners pay CPF on bonus?

Foreign employees who are not Singapore Citizens or PRs generally do not contribute CPF, so CPF usually does not apply to their bonus.

Can I get bonus if I resign before payout?

It depends on your bonus terms. Some companies require employees to still be employed on the payout date or not be serving notice.

How is prorated bonus calculated?

A simple prorated bonus estimate is monthly salary × bonus months × months worked ÷ 12. Actual company policy may use a different method.

Does bonus affect income tax?

Yes. Bonus increases your annual employment income, which may increase your income tax depending on your total chargeable income and tax residency.
