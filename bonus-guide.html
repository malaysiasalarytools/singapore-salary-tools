Singapore bonus guide for employees

Understand how bonus, AWS, 13th month payment, CPF on bonus, income tax and prorated bonus work in Singapore. Useful for employees comparing job offers, planning resignation or checking year-end salary.

What is a bonus in Singapore?

A bonus is an additional payment given to employees on top of their basic salary. It may be paid as a year-end bonus, performance bonus, company bonus, variable bonus, retention bonus or Annual Wage Supplement commonly known as AWS or 13th month payment.

In Singapore, bonus payments are generally not automatic unless they are stated in your employment contract, collective agreement, company policy or official bonus letter. If the bonus is discretionary, the employer usually has more flexibility to decide whether to pay it and how much to pay.

Simple rule: Check your employment contract first. If the bonus or AWS is clearly promised in writing, it is stronger than a verbal expectation or past practice.

Is AWS or 13th month bonus compulsory in Singapore?

AWS is also called the 13th month payment. It is a single annual payment made on top of an employee’s normal annual salary. AWS is not compulsory by default. It depends on what is stated in your employment contract, collective agreement or company policy.

If your contract says you are entitled to AWS, your employer should follow the agreed terms. If your contract does not mention AWS, the company may still choose to pay it, but it is usually treated as discretionary.

Example: If your monthly salary is S$4,000 and your company pays 1 month AWS, your AWS is usually S$4,000 before CPF and tax treatment.

Is performance bonus compulsory?

Performance bonus is usually not compulsory unless the contract or bonus scheme gives you a clear entitlement. Many companies use wording such as “discretionary bonus”, “subject to company performance” or “subject to management approval”. This means the final amount may depend on employer decision.

However, if the bonus is clearly guaranteed in your offer letter or employment contract, you should keep a copy of that document because it helps support your claim if there is a dispute.

Is CPF payable on bonus?

CPF is generally payable on bonus for Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents who are eligible for CPF. Bonus is usually treated as Additional Wages for CPF purposes. This means it may be subject to the CPF Additional Wage ceiling.

The exact CPF amount depends on your age, citizenship or PR status, total Ordinary Wages for the year and the Additional Wage ceiling. For a quick estimate, you can use a CPF calculator, but the official CPF rules should be checked for payroll accuracy.

Important: Bonus CPF is not always simply 20% of the full bonus. CPF wage ceilings can affect how much of the bonus attracts CPF contribution.

Is bonus taxable in Singapore?

Bonus is generally part of employment income and may be taxable in Singapore. This includes AWS, performance bonus, incentive bonus and other employment-related bonus payments.

For tax residents, bonus is usually added to annual employment income and taxed based on the resident progressive income tax rates after allowable deductions and reliefs. For non-residents, tax treatment may differ depending on tax residency and employment arrangement.

Do you get bonus if you resign?

Whether you receive bonus after resignation depends on the bonus terms. Some companies require employees to be in active employment on the bonus payment date. Others may require employees not to be serving notice at the time of bonus declaration or payout.

If your bonus is discretionary, resigning before the bonus is declared or paid may reduce your chance of receiving it. If your bonus is guaranteed or contractually earned, the answer depends on the exact wording.

  • Check whether the bonus is guaranteed or discretionary.
  • Check whether you must still be employed on the payout date.
  • Check whether employees serving notice are excluded.
  • Check whether the bonus is prorated for incomplete service.

How does prorated bonus work?

Prorated bonus means the bonus is calculated based on the period you worked during the bonus year. For example, if the company pays 1 month AWS and you worked 6 months in the year, a simple prorated AWS may be half a month of salary.

Simple prorated bonus formula: Monthly salary × bonus months × months worked ÷ 12

For example, if your salary is S$4,000, the company pays 1 month bonus, and you worked 9 months, a simple prorated bonus estimate is S$4,000 × 1 × 9 ÷ 12 = S$3,000.

Actual bonus calculation may be different if your company uses appraisal rating, company performance factor, bonus pool allocation or specific HR policy.

Common bonus mistakes employees make

Assuming bonus is automatic

Many bonuses are discretionary unless clearly stated in the contract.

CPF on bonus may be affected by Additional Wage ceiling rules.

Resigning too early

Some companies require employees to remain employed until payout date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is bonus compulsory in Singapore?

Bonus is generally not compulsory unless it is stated in your employment contract, collective agreement or company policy.

Is AWS the same as 13th month bonus?

Yes. AWS is commonly known as the 13th month payment. It is a single annual payment on top of normal salary.

Is AWS compulsory in Singapore?

AWS is not compulsory by default. It depends on your employment contract, collective agreement or company policy.

Is CPF payable on bonus?

CPF is generally payable on bonus for CPF-eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs, subject to CPF wage ceiling rules.

Is bonus taxable in Singapore?

Bonus is generally treated as employment income and may be taxable as part of your annual income.

Do foreigners pay CPF on bonus?

Foreign employees who are not Singapore Citizens or PRs generally do not contribute CPF, so CPF usually does not apply to their bonus.

Can I get bonus if I resign before payout?

It depends on your bonus terms. Some companies require employees to still be employed on the payout date or not be serving notice.

How is prorated bonus calculated?

A simple prorated bonus estimate is monthly salary × bonus months × months worked ÷ 12. Actual company policy may use a different method.

Does bonus affect income tax?

Yes. Bonus increases your annual employment income, which may increase your income tax depending on your total chargeable income and tax residency.

Is this bonus guide official advice?

No. This guide is for general salary planning only. Check MOM, CPF Board, IRAS, your employment contract or HR policy for official treatment.
