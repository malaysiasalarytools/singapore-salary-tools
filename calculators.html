Frequently Asked Questions

Which Singapore salary calculator should I use first?

Start with the Singapore Salary Calculator on the home page. It estimates monthly take-home pay after CPF and income tax.

Are these calculators official?

No. These calculators provide estimates only. For official CPF, income tax or employment rules, refer to CPF Board, IRAS and MOM.

Does the salary calculator include CPF?

Yes. The main salary calculator estimates employee CPF and employer CPF for eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs.

Can foreigners use the calculators?

Yes. Foreigners can use the no CPF option in the salary calculator where CPF does not apply.

Are these calculators free?

Yes. All calculators on SG Salary Tools are free to use.
