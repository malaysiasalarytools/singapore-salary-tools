How salary after tax is calculated

Salary after tax means your income after employee CPF contribution and estimated personal income tax. CPF affects your monthly cash salary, while income tax is usually assessed annually.

1. Start with gross income

Your annual gross income includes monthly salary multiplied by 12, plus any annual bonus entered.

2. Deduct CPF and reliefs

Employee CPF and tax reliefs reduce the amount used to estimate chargeable income.

3. Estimate income tax

The calculator applies Singapore progressive income tax rates to estimate annual tax payable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is salary after tax in Singapore?

Salary after tax is your income after employee CPF contribution and estimated personal income tax. It gives a clearer view of your real take-home pay.

Is CPF considered a tax in Singapore?

CPF is not income tax. It is a mandatory social security savings contribution for eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs. However, employee CPF reduces your monthly cash salary.

Does this calculator include CPF?

Yes. You can choose CPF eligibility and age group to estimate employee CPF deduction. You can also select no CPF if CPF does not apply to your employment situation.

How is Singapore income tax calculated?

For tax residents, Singapore income tax is calculated using progressive tax rates on chargeable income. Chargeable income is estimated after deducting CPF and tax reliefs.

Do foreigners pay salary tax in Singapore?

Foreigners working in Singapore may still pay income tax. The tax treatment depends on whether they are treated as tax residents or non-residents.

Is income tax deducted from monthly salary in Singapore?

Singapore income tax is usually assessed annually rather than deducted monthly like payroll tax. This calculator spreads estimated annual tax over 12 months for planning.

Why is my actual tax different from this calculator?

Actual tax may differ because of tax reliefs, rebates, deductions, CPF treatment, bonuses, employment benefits and IRAS assessment rules.

Can I use this calculator for job offer comparison?

Yes. It is useful for comparing gross salary, CPF deduction, annual tax and estimated net salary before accepting a job offer.
