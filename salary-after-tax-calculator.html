What Is Salary After Tax in Singapore?

Salary after tax means your estimated income after deducting employee CPF contributions and Singapore personal income tax. For Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, CPF reduces your monthly cash salary, while income tax is usually assessed annually.

Salary After Tax Example

If your monthly salary is S$5,000 and you are aged 55 and below, the calculator estimates employee CPF at 20% of salary, subject to the CPF Ordinary Wage ceiling. It then estimates your annual chargeable income and applies Singapore resident tax rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is salary after tax the same as take-home pay?

They are similar, but take-home pay usually refers to your monthly cash salary after CPF. Salary after tax also considers the estimated annual income tax impact.

Does this calculator include CPF?

Yes. It estimates employee CPF based on your salary and age group.

Does Singapore deduct tax from salary every month?

Usually no. Singapore income tax is generally assessed annually, so this calculator spreads estimated annual tax across 12 months for easier comparison.

Can foreigners use this calculator?

This page is mainly designed for Singapore tax residents who contribute CPF. Foreign employees may have different CPF and tax treatment.
