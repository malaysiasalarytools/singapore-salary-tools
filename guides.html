Frequently Asked Questions

What topics do the Singapore salary guides cover?

The guides cover CPF, payroll, employment rights, leave, overtime, working hours, notice periods, personal income tax, bonuses, AWS and salary planning.

Are these guides official government advice?

No. The guides provide general information only. For official CPF, tax and employment requirements, refer to CPF Board, IRAS and MOM.

Can I use the guides together with the calculators?

Yes. The guides explain the rules and assumptions behind many of the calculators available on SG Salary Tools.
