Frequently Asked Questions

Are these official Singapore government guides?

No. These guides explain common Singapore salary, CPF, tax and employment topics in simple language. For official rules, always refer to CPF Board, IRAS and MOM.

Who are these guides for?

These guides are for employees, job seekers, payroll users, HR users and anyone who wants to understand salary planning in Singapore.

Which guide should I read first?

Start with the CPF Contribution Rates Guide, Income Tax Guide and Tax Relief Guide. These explain the main items used in the salary calculator.

Are the guides free?

Yes. All guides and calculators on SG Salary Tools are free to use.

How often are the guides updated?

The guides are reviewed when major CPF, income tax or employment rule changes affect salary planning.
