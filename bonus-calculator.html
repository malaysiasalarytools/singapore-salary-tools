How this Singapore bonus calculator works

A bonus is usually treated as additional employment income. This calculator estimates your gross bonus, CPF on bonus, tax impact from bonus, and final estimated bonus after CPF and tax.

1. Calculate gross bonus

Gross bonus is calculated from bonus months plus any fixed bonus amount. For example, S$5,000 salary × 1 month = S$5,000 bonus.

2. Estimate CPF on bonus

Bonus is usually treated as Additional Wages for CPF. CPF on bonus is subject to the Additional Wage ceiling.

3. Estimate tax impact

Bonus is generally taxable. The calculator estimates extra tax by comparing your tax before and after bonus.

What is AWS or 13th month bonus?

AWS stands for Annual Wage Supplement. It is commonly called 13th month bonus in Singapore. It is usually based on one month of basic salary, but whether you are entitled to AWS depends on your employment contract, company policy or collective agreement.

1 month AWS

If your monthly salary is S$5,000 and your AWS is 1 month, your gross AWS amount is S$5,000.

Performance bonus

Performance bonus may be based on company performance, personal performance or management decision.

Fixed bonus

Some employees receive a fixed contractual bonus amount instead of a bonus based on months of salary.

CPF on bonus in Singapore

Bonuses are usually Additional Wages for CPF. CPF on Additional Wages is limited by the Additional Wage ceiling. The simplified formula used by this calculator is:

Bonus and income tax

Bonus is generally taxable employment income in Singapore. This calculator estimates the tax impact of your bonus by calculating annual income tax before and after bonus, then showing the difference as estimated tax impact.

Non-contractual bonus

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate bonus in Singapore?

A simple bonus calculation is monthly salary multiplied by bonus months, plus any fixed bonus amount. For example, S$5,000 salary with 1 month bonus gives a gross bonus of S$5,000.

Is bonus taxable in Singapore?

Yes. Employment bonus is generally taxable in Singapore. This includes common forms of bonus such as AWS, 13th month payment, contractual bonus and performance bonus.

Is CPF payable on bonus?

CPF may be payable on bonus if CPF applies to your employment. Bonus is usually treated as Additional Wages and is subject to the CPF Additional Wage ceiling.

What is the Additional Wage ceiling?

The Additional Wage ceiling limits how much bonus or Additional Wages attract CPF. It is generally calculated as S$102,000 minus total Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year.

What is AWS in Singapore?

AWS means Annual Wage Supplement. It is often called 13th month bonus. It is not automatically guaranteed for every employee unless it is provided under the employment contract, company policy or collective agreement.

How much is 1 month bonus after CPF?

For an employee aged 55 and below under full CPF rates, employee CPF is generally 20% on CPF-subject bonus. A S$5,000 bonus may become around S$4,000 after employee CPF before income tax.

Do foreigners pay CPF on bonus in Singapore?

Foreign employees generally do not contribute CPF. If CPF does not apply, choose the no CPF option in this calculator. Tax may still apply depending on tax residency and Singapore employment income.

Does this calculator include income tax?

Yes. It estimates the additional income tax caused by bonus by comparing estimated annual tax before bonus and after bonus. Actual IRAS assessment may differ.

Is this bonus calculator official?

No. This calculator provides estimates only. For official CPF and tax treatment, check CPF Board and IRAS directly.
