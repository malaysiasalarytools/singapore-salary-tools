How this Singapore salary calculator works

1. Start with gross salary

Your gross monthly salary is the amount before CPF, income tax and other deductions.

2. Estimate CPF

Employee CPF is deducted from your salary. Employer CPF is paid by your employer on top of your salary, subject to CPF rules and wage ceilings.

3. Estimate tax

Singapore income tax is calculated annually. This calculator spreads the estimated annual tax over 12 months to show a monthly view.

FAQ

Is CPF calculated on the full salary?

Not always. CPF Ordinary Wage contributions are subject to a monthly wage ceiling. For 2026, this calculator uses an Ordinary Wage ceiling of $8,000.

Is income tax deducted monthly in Singapore?

Usually, Singapore income tax is assessed annually by IRAS. This calculator shows a monthly estimate so users can understand their practical take-home pay.

Can foreigners use this calculator?

Yes, but foreigners who are not Singapore Citizens or PRs usually do not contribute CPF. For better accuracy, you may create a separate foreign employee version later.
