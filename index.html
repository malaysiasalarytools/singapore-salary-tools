How Salary Is Calculated in Singapore

Calculating your take-home pay in Singapore means starting from your gross monthly salary, then subtracting employee CPF contribution and estimated income tax. Employer CPF is paid separately by your employer and does not reduce your cash salary.

Take-Home Pay = Gross Salary − Employee CPF − Estimated Income Tax Employer CPF is shown separately because it is not deducted from your salary.

Tax Relief & Salary Notes

Click each item below to understand common Singapore salary and tax planning points.

Earned Income Relief

Tax residents may qualify for earned income relief depending on age and eligibility. This calculator lets you enter your estimated annual reliefs manually.

CPF Relief

Employee CPF contributions may reduce taxable income for eligible employees. Actual relief treatment depends on IRAS rules and your personal situation.

Parent Relief

Qualifying taxpayers may claim parent relief if they support eligible parents or grandparents.

Child Relief

Married taxpayers with qualifying children may be eligible for child-related tax reliefs.

Course Fees Relief

Bonus & Annual Income

Bonuses increase annual income and may increase estimated income tax. This calculator includes bonus for tax estimate, but keeps monthly CPF simple.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this Singapore salary calculator include CPF and income tax?

Yes. This calculator estimates employee CPF contribution, employer CPF contribution, annual income tax and monthly take-home pay.

How do I calculate take-home pay in Singapore?

Take-home pay is your gross monthly salary minus employee CPF contribution and estimated monthly income tax. Employer CPF is paid separately and does not reduce your cash salary.

Do foreigners need to contribute CPF in Singapore?

Many foreigners working in Singapore do not contribute CPF. This calculator includes a No CPF option for foreign employees.

Is income tax deducted monthly in Singapore?

Singapore income tax is generally assessed annually. This calculator spreads estimated annual tax over 12 months for salary planning.

Why is my actual salary different from the calculator result?

Actual payroll may differ because of CPF eligibility, PR year status, bonuses, allowances, tax reliefs and employer payroll settings.

Is S$5,000 salary after CPF around S$4,000?

For an employee aged 55 and below with CPF, employee CPF is usually around 20% of eligible monthly wages. On S$5,000 salary, the take-home before income tax is around S$4,000.
