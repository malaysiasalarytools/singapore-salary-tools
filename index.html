How this calculator works

Your gross salary may be reduced by employee CPF contribution before you receive your monthly cash salary. Singapore income tax is assessed annually, so this calculator spreads the estimated annual tax over 12 months to give a practical take-home pay estimate.

1. Gross salary

Your gross monthly salary is the amount before CPF, tax and other deductions.

2. CPF contribution

Employee CPF is deducted from salary for eligible employees. Employer CPF is paid separately by the employer.

3. Income tax

The calculator estimates annual income tax and converts it into a monthly estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate take-home pay in Singapore?

Take-home pay is your gross salary minus employee CPF contributions and estimated income tax. This calculator estimates both to show your approximate net salary.

Does this calculator include CPF contributions?

Yes. It estimates both employee CPF deductions and employer CPF contributions for eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs. You can also choose the no CPF option if CPF does not apply to your employment situation.

Do foreigners in Singapore need to contribute CPF?

Many foreigners working in Singapore do not contribute CPF. If CPF does not apply to you, choose the foreigner / no CPF option so the calculator will estimate your salary without CPF deduction.

How much CPF do employees contribute in Singapore?

CPF contribution rates vary by age. Employees aged 55 and below generally contribute up to 20% of eligible wages.

How much CPF does my employer contribute?

Employer CPF is paid separately by your employer and does not reduce your salary. The amount depends on your age, salary and CPF eligibility.

Is CPF calculated on the full salary?

CPF contributions are subject to CPF wage ceilings. If your salary exceeds the Ordinary Wage ceiling, CPF will only be calculated up to the applicable limit.

Is income tax deducted monthly in Singapore?

Singapore income tax is usually assessed annually. This calculator converts estimated annual tax into a monthly figure for planning.

Is S$5,000 a good salary in Singapore?

S$5,000 can be comfortable for a single person depending on rent, lifestyle and savings goals. After employee CPF, take-home before tax may be around S$4,000.

Can foreigners use this salary calculator?

Yes. Foreigners can use the no CPF option to estimate salary without CPF deduction. Tax treatment may still depend on tax residency and your employment arrangement.

How accurate is this Singapore Salary Calculator?

It provides an estimate only. Actual results may vary depending on CPF eligibility, tax reliefs, deductions, bonuses and employment arrangements.
